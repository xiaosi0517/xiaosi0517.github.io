# Becoming Me

This project is an ongoing personal experiment in using AI not just to speed work, but to help a person think more independently, understand themselves more deeply, and live a richer life over time.

## What this is about

I’m exploring AI as a long-term companion for growth—one that:

- encourages curiosity instead of spoon-feeding answers
- challenges assumptions without being confrontational
- helps people reflect on how they change over time
- makes room for nuance, uncertainty, and self-directed learning

## Why it matters

Most AI tools are designed for productivity. This one asks a different question: can AI support a human’s own journey, not just their tasks?

This project is framed as a personal lab where the design, the experience, and the values are all being tested together.

A public project page and formal project link will follow soon. For now, this portfolio entry serves as the first introduction to the idea and the ongoing experiment.
